Balanced serial trials · identical correctness gates

Measured runtime, without a general speed claim.

Seven measured balanced serial trials each compared original 176, final 200 and the installed Chromium: 21 trials total, all passing the same 9,395 correctness checks. There were no separately launched warmup trials; three media-only native captures preceded the run.

The ranges overlap substantially. These measurements do not establish a stable broad Lumen speedup, and Chromium was faster on this workload.

Resource values are the median of each trial’s sample median, then the median of seven trial medians. Units are decimal MB (1,000,000 bytes). Sampling was once per second over each owned browser process tree, including startup, work and cleanup. They are not medians of peak memory or idle-browser measurements.

Summed RSS double-counts shared mappings; PSS is the proportional sampled quantity. Native trials had four memory samples and three CPU intervals; Chromium had three memory samples and two CPU intervals. Full ranges, individual trials and observed maxima are retained in the JSON report.

Final 200 was faster in 4/7 DOM, 5/7 style/layout and 5/7 grid pairs, with overlapping ranges. Both browsers used the documented software/offscreen paths. Intel adapter enumeration did not establish actual Lumen hardware rendering.

Actual native exits were 0, all seven Chromium owned identity lists were empty, and every owned profile was removed. The timing run ended at 20:38:58 UTC before the final broad conformance cohort began.
